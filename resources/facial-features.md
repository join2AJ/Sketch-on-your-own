# Drawing the Facial Features

How to draw eyes, eyebrows, nose, lips/mouth and ears — distilled from beginner
tutorials and YouTube lessons. Used in the app two ways:

1. **Better rendering.** Faces are smoothly lit, so the eyes, nostrils and lip line
   are *low-contrast* and a plain line pass misses them. We apply **local contrast
   enhancement** inside the detected face (an unsharp/CLAHE-style boost) so those
   subtle features turn into real lines.
2. **Feature-specific instructions.** When a grid cell falls on a known feature
   (placed via Loomis proportions), the written step teaches *that feature* using
   the tips below — instead of a generic "draw some lines."

---

## Proportions that place the features

- **Eyes** sit about **halfway down** the head, and are **one eye-width apart**.
- **Nose width** ≈ the distance between the **inner corners of the eyes**.
- **Mouth width** ≈ lines dropped from the **centers/inner edges of the eyes**.
- **Ears** run from about the **brow line down to the nose line**.
- **Eyebrows** sit just above the eye sockets, on the brow line.

---

## 👁️ Eyes

The eye is a **sphere in a socket**, not a flat almond.

1. Lightly mark a horizontal line (both eyes) and a vertical center for each.
2. Draw the **almond outline** — corners slightly pointed.
3. Add a **circle for the iris** and a smaller **dark pupil** inside it. The iris is
   partly hidden by the upper lid.
4. **Leave a tiny white highlight** touching the pupil/iris — this is what makes an
   eye look *alive*. Put it in the **same place in both eyes**.
5. **Darken under the upper lid** (it casts a shadow on the eye); the upper lash
   line is the boldest edge. Add lashes last, in the growth direction.

## 〰️ Eyebrows

A **band of short strokes** following the brow, in the hair-growth direction —
usually a little **darker/thicker at the inner end**, tapering out.

## 👃 Nose

**Shade, don't outline.** Heavy outlines make a nose look fake.

1. The **bridge** starts between the eyes and slopes down.
2. The **tip** is a rounded ball; the **nostrils** read as two soft triangular
   shadows.
3. Suggest the form with **shading on the sides and under the tip**; keep the width
   to the inner-eye-corner spacing.

## 👄 Lips / Mouth

1. Start with the **center line where the lips meet** — this is the **darkest** line
   and the most important; it's not straight, it gently waves.
2. The **upper lip** is usually **thinner**, shaped like a soft **"M" / cupid's bow**,
   and tends to be **darker** (it faces away from the light).
3. The **lower lip** is **fuller** and **catches light**, so leave a **highlight** on it.
4. **Darken the corners** of the mouth; avoid a hard outline around the whole lips.

## 👂 Ears

A **"C" shape** with folds inside (the rim/helix, the inner ridge, and the earlobe).
Vertically it spans roughly **brow line → nose line**. Simplest read: the outer C,
one inner curved fold, a **dark shadow in the bowl**, and a soft lobe.
