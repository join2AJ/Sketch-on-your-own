All steps

    How it works

    1. Upload a photo. It stays in your browser — nothing is sent to a server.
    2. We lay a grid over it — the classic artist's technique for copying proportions accurately.
    3. We trace the edges of your photo to figure out the sketch lines.
    4. Draw the same grid lightly on your paper (same number of rows & columns).
    5. Follow each step. For every grid cell you'll get a written instruction and an animation showing exactly what lines go there.
    6. Erase the grid at the end — and you've drawn the photo!

    💡 Tip: Draw the grid on your paper very lightly in pencil so it's easy to erase later.
